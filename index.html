Meet Ray & Sally

We’re a husband-and-wife duo and the founders of Pneuma & Co., a faith-led creative studio inspired by pneuma—the breath, wind, and Spirit of God. We believe the Holy Spirit is our true creative partner, and the “& Co.” represents the two of us, Ray and Sally.

We believe creativity is a gift from God, and we create with peace, intention, excellence, and authenticity at the center.

Whether we’re bringing a brand’s story to life, creating content that helps a business grow, documenting a wedding, or capturing family memories, our hope is that you feel seen, cared for, and at peace throughout the process.

That’s the heart behind Pneuma & Co.Ray & Sally Simpson
